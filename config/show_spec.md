# Show Specification — Lehigh Valley Morning Briefing

## Show Name

Lehigh Valley Morning Briefing

## Audience

Residents of the Lehigh Valley (Allentown, Bethlehem, Easton, and surrounding
Lehigh and Northampton County communities) who want a quick local catch-up
before starting their day.

## Tone

- Conversational and clear — plain language, no jargon.
- Neutral — informative, not cheerful-DJ and not dramatic-anchor.
- Efficient — every sentence earns its place.

## Target Runtime

- **Goal:** ~5 minutes (roughly 700–800 words of script).
- **Hard ceiling:** 7 minutes. If material exceeds this, cut lower-priority items.

## Core Sections (in order)

| # | Section            | Target time | Description                                                |
|---|--------------------|-----------:|------------------------------------------------------------|
| 1 | Local News         | 90 sec      | Top 2–4 stories from Lehigh Valley outlets.                |
| 2 | Weather            | 45 sec      | Today's forecast, high/low, precipitation, any alerts.     |
| 3 | Traffic & Transit  | 30 sec      | Major commute disruptions; skip if nothing notable.        |
| 4 | Local Government   | 45 sec      | Council actions, public meetings, official decisions.      |
| 5 | Community          | 30 sec      | Events, school news, things worth knowing today.           |

Section 3 (Traffic) and Section 5 (Community) may be omitted on days when there
is nothing meaningful to report. Sections 1, 2, and 4 always appear.

## Exclusions

- National or international news unless it has a direct, concrete local impact.
- Sports scores and recaps.
- Opinion, commentary, or editorial takes.
- Sponsored content, advertising, or promotion.
- Crime blotter items with no broader public-safety relevance.
- Rumors, unverified social-media claims, or single-anonymous-source stories.

## Human Review Requirement

Every episode **must** be reviewed and approved by a human editor before
publication. No episode is auto-published. The review step covers:

1. Factual accuracy — claims match cited sources.
2. Source attribution — every fact is traceable.
3. Tone — neutral, no inadvertent editorializing.
4. Completeness — no major local story obviously missing.
